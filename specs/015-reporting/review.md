# Review: 015 reporting — the brand's reporting service, and the producer amendments it needs

**Verdict:** Request changes
**What it does:** Adds `services/reporting`, which folds the brand's facts into intake entities, a `BrandDay` per reporting day and views. From those it serves the original's Reports tab, the dashboard and the dispute report, plus the new day-end, compliance exports, prediction-market reports and document lifecycle. It also amends the contract and five producers (players, game-provider, wallet, merchant, psp-gateway) so that test accounts, game names, chargebacks, wagering progress and taker fees exist as facts.
**Should it exist:** yes. FR-001 forbids figures from request-time calls, and the original sampled twenty players per request. Folding facts per brand is the right layer, and putting the missing producer fields on facts (not on reads of other services' stores) follows constitution IV.
**Read:** spec.md (FRs, SCs), plan.md, tasks.md, and research.md R4, R8, R9 and *Found during implementation*. Every producer hunk in full: contract facts, wallet (`ServiceEndpoint`, `PaymentEntity`, `publish/Facts`), merchant (`CaptureRoutes`, `Endpoints`, `Payment`, `OutcomeReporter`, `WalletClient`, `AuditFacts`), psp-gateway (`DepositSession`, `Outcome`, `OutcomeDeliverer`, `Receivers`, `Webhooks`, `UnityFinance`, `SessionsView`), players (`OperatorProfileRoutes`, `PlayerEntity`, `Facts`) and game-provider (`Callbacks`, `Round`, `RoundModel`, `RoundFacts`). In reporting, read in full: `ReportingService`, `ReportingRuntime`, `api/{ReportingEndpoint,Access,Reports,Disputes,Documents}`, the tail of `api/DayEnd`, `intake/{WalletBook,FactId,Generation,Coverage}`, `day/{BrandDay,ToDays,CloseDays,DayEndInputs,DaysView}`, `days/Days`, `documents/{Document,DocumentJob,Erasure,Keeping,Sweep}` and `reads/Transactions`. Skimmed, read by the identifiers the findings touch: `api/{Dashboard,Exports,Jobs,Predictions,Compliance,Wire}`, `render/DisputeReport`, the remaining `reads/*`, `intake/*` and `predictions/*`, and `Settings`. Tests read: `it/RevenueSuite` (SC-004), `it/Kit.rebuild`, and `perf/PerfSuite`'s seeding. CI, the Justfile, `build.sbt`, `service.json` and `module.json` were read. Outside the diff, `merchant/api/Endpoints.scala` was opened for the dispute route's ACL. Not reviewed line by line: the 31 recordings and `suite-result.json` (generated data), the Go recording driver (skimmed for scope; it is test tooling that runs only against kind), and most of the remaining 90 test files.
**Size:** 279 files, +35,223 −323. About 15.5k of those are hand-written Scala and about 15.6k are recording JSON. Defect-finding falls off well before this size. The split we would have accepted: (1) the contract and the five producer amendments, which touch real-money services and deserve their own review; (2) the reporting service; (3) the driver, the recordings and the parity tests. We do not ask for a split now, because the branch is built story by story with Conventional Commits that can be read one at a time. The producer hunks should still get a second reader from each service's owner.

## Blocking

- `services/reporting/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/reporting/day/CloseDays.scala:50`: issue (blocking). `close-days` closes every open day once its end plus `CLOSE_GRACE` has passed, without asking whether the intake and `to-days` consumers have read that far. A day is therefore fixed with partial sections whenever the consumers are behind at the close. Three cases make this happen:
  - a new `REPORTING_GENERATION`, the designed recovery path (R8). It replays the topics from the earliest offset, and each past day is closed within five minutes of its first fold;
  - the first deployment over topics that already hold history;
  - any downtime or lag of reporting that spans midnight plus 15 minutes. At restart the timer closes yesterday before the backlog is read.

  Every fact folded after that point becomes a `Corrected`. The revenue report survives because `Revenue.daySection` adds corrections back (research, T035–T036). The day-end does not: `DayEnd.of` renders the `DayFixed` sections, so its deposits, games, GGR and chargebacks are partial. `liabilityAtClose` and `pendingHoldsAtClose` are read from today's views (`DayEndInputs.gather`). The games and differences come from a `Rounds` view that is itself still being rebuilt. The replayed facts are all dated `arrivedAt = now`, so the latest day-end's `openingCorrections` fills with the whole history. This breaks FR-018 (the day-end's figures), SC-004 (rebuild and rerun every report: identical figures) and SC-008's meaning. No suite catches it, because `RevenueSuite`'s SC-004 test compares only `/reports/ggr`.
  Suggestion: close a day only past a watermark. Each intake consumer records the latest `occurredAt` it has folded as well as the earliest. Change `Coverage` to `earliest` + `latest`, or add a sibling entity, and have the `to-days` folders record the last source time they folded. `DayClosing.run` then closes `day` only when every topic in the generation with traffic has a `latest` at or after `endOf(day) + grace`. As a backstop for a quiet topic, also accept a wall-clock bound well past the grace. Add a `DayEndSuite` case that publishes a day, closes it, rebuilds into a new generation, and asserts that `GET /day-end/{day}` is byte-identical in its sections and that today's opening holds no replayed corrections. Until a watermark exists, `/status` should not report `rebuilding: false` for a generation still catching up (research T031 records the ankka gap; the watermark above also answers it).
  Resolved: a day closes only once every to-days consumer has read past its end plus the grace, or has been idle for `CLOSE_IDLE` (15m), recorded per generation in `FoldProgress` (decided 2026-11-09, research.md); DayEndSuite rebuilds into a new generation and compares the day-end, 2f2ae2fd, f7764fa5, b0ae61b7, 11233ce9

## Should fix

- `services/game-provider/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/gameprovider/api/callbacks/Callbacks.scala:112-120`: issue (non-blocking). `opening` now reads the `Game` key value entity on every debit, credit, buyin and payout callback. That is an extra entity round trip on ST8's latency-bound money path, and the result is used only when the callback opens a new round. The ticket already carries `gameCategory` (`t.gameCategory`, passed to `BetJob` two lines above). Suggestion: take `gameType` from the ticket, and read the catalogue's name only when the round is new. For example, the `Round` entity could fill `gameName` itself at `RoundOpened`, or the opening could be built lazily inside the job's first step. Otherwise state the added latency with a number from the game-provider perf suite.
  Resolved: the catalogue is read only when a round opens (the round asks for the opening, and the workflow reads it); not from the ticket, whose game can differ from a debit's `game_code`; a callback on an open round reads nothing, 77a257ac

- `services/reporting/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/reporting/day/ToDays.scala:113`: issue (non-blocking). A delta's test-account mark is read from `PlayerLog` at fold time. `PlayerLog` is fed by another consumer (`players-facts`) on another topic, so the mark depends on which consumer ran first. A test account's deposits and bets folded before `players-facts` has read its `player_registered` are counted as real players'. The same facts can give different "excluding test accounts" figures in two generations, which breaks SC-004 whenever `excludeTestAccounts` is on. Suggestion: when `PlayerLog` has not yet seen the player's registration (its state has no player), fail the fold so it is redelivered, bounded by a few retries or by the registration's coverage. Add a `RevenueSuite` case that publishes a test account's money before its registration and asserts the excluded figures.
  Resolved: money folded before the registration is held in `TestHolds` and re-folded into the test sections when the registration marks a test account, so the figures are the same whichever consumer ran first, 6ef9d394

- `services/reporting/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/reporting/api/Reports.scala:213` and `services/reporting/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/reporting/documents/DocumentJob.scala:95-103`: issue (non-blocking). Both export paths hold the whole result in memory. The JSON export reads every matching ledger row into one `Vector` (`Transactions.all`, no cap) for a brand-wide period. The job's `store` step joins every page into one `String`, then one `Array[Byte]`. That contradicts the class comment ("so neither the journal nor memory holds the rows"). A month of a real brand's ledger is millions of rows. Suggestion: refuse or redirect the JSON export over `syncRows`, as the CSV path does, and have `store` stream the parts into the object, which needs a multipart or append on `Bucket` (an ankka finding if the API has neither). At minimum, correct the comment and state the bound.
  Resolved: the store step streams the parts one at a time; the JSON ledger export is chunked, 10,000 rows at a time by key; a dispute report still joins its parts, being rendered whole (the comment and R9 say so), 2d81fa24, 90e5bf6a

- `services/reporting/src/test/scala/io/eitheror/casino/reporting/perf/PerfSuite.scala:23-50` with `reads/Transactions.scala:41-47`: suggestion. The performance evidence measures players, not movements. Each of the 10,000 players plays one round on one day, about 40k legs a month. Every view filters and orders on `payload::jsonb->>…` expressions, and the diff declares no index for any of them. The ledger pages with `OFFSET` over all generations' rows, and every money leg is one persist on a single `BrandDay` entity per day. At a real brand's volume (hundreds of bets a minute), both the reads behind SC-001–SC-003 and the day entity's write rate are unmeasured. Suggestion: run `reporting-perf` with a leg count from Spinvibe's production month and attach the timings. Attach `EXPLAIN` output for `Transactions.between` and `DaysView.between`, and add expression indexes where the view API allows them, or record their absence as an ankka finding.
  Resolved: the perf suite plays rounds per player (100 by default, 1M bets a month over 10,000 players) with EXPLAIN ANALYZE; at 10,000 players x 10 rounds the rankings missed SC-002 and now sum in the view (332 ms), revenue 26-55 ms, other dashboard routes under 170 ms. ankka 0.16 views cannot declare indexes (an ankka feature); the full 1.5M-leg month did not finish (ankka's projection layer slowed to ~20 legs/s), recorded in R17, 5eaf845c

- `services/reporting/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/reporting/reads/Players.scala:84,96,100`, `reads/Wallets.scala:65,68` and `day/DaysView.scala:82`: issue (non-blocking) (if-minor). The reads cap their results silently at 100,000 or 1,000,000 rows. The spec's central promise is "every player, not a sample". A cap that truncates without saying so reintroduces sampling at scale. Suggestion: one named constant per read, and a log line or a refusal when a read returns exactly the cap.
  Resolved: unbounded reads page by key to their end; bounded ones read one past the bound and refuse with a logged `TooManyRows`, fb3f1b56

- `services/reporting/build.sbt:62`: suggestion (if-minor). `com.openhtmltopdf:openhtmltopdf-pdfbox:1.0.10` is the unmaintained original coordinate (last release 2021), and it pulls pdfbox 2.0.24, a line that is at end of life. The maintained fork is `io.github.openhtmltopdf:openhtmltopdf-pdfbox` 1.1.x, with the same API under `com.openhtmltopdf`. The risk is low, because the service only writes PDFs from HTML it has escaped, but a new dependency should start on a maintained line.
  Resolved: `io.github.openhtmltopdf:openhtmltopdf-pdfbox` 1.1.100 (released 2026-10-22), on PDFBox 3.0.8, 8f20e2bf

## Praise

- `services/reporting/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/reporting/documents/DocumentJob.scala:90-113` with `Document.readied`: praise. The race between a job and an erasure is closed at both ends. `store` checks the keyring for an erased player before keeping anything, and `readied` refuses to move a record out of `expired`, so the object written in the gap is dropped. Pages live under the subject's prefix, so `bucket.erase` takes them too. This is the hard part of FR-021 done properly.
- `services/psp-gateway/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/pspgateway/sessions/DepositSession.scala:76-88` (`current`, `deliverable(dispute)`): praise. Disputes queue behind the capture's delivery, then behind each other. `merchant` therefore never hears of a chargeback for a payment it has not credited, and the re-drive and the listing follow the delivery that is current. The existing outcome path is unchanged for sessions with no dispute.
- `services/reporting/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/reporting/intake/Generation.scala` and `intake/FactId.scala`: praise. Rebuild by generation is one function for every entity id and component id, and the dedup id is derived deterministically from the contract's bytes. The rebuild model is easy to reason about, and the finding above is about when a day closes, not about this mechanism.
- `services/wallet/src/main/scala/io/eitheror/casino/wallet/publish/Facts.scala:217-222`: praise. A closed dispute's amount and currency are published both-or-neither, so a reader never takes an amount without its currency.
