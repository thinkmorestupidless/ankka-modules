# Review: spec 018, the shared audit (a0f13277...HEAD)

**Verdict:** Request changes
**What it does:** Adds `services/audit`, the one service of the shared `reasoner` project. It consumes each brand's `platform.audit` and `platform.modules` and `payments`' `platform.audit` as bytes. It keeps action records and passes over unattended money events, replaces people's ids with registry pseudonyms, and stores each record once in a key value entity with a view. It serves queries, exports, re-identification, role grants, retention and status, and records each of them. The change also amends the contract (`Routes`, `Layout`), the realm, the modules tool, both shared manifests, and the audit mappings of `wallet`, `players` and `responsible-gambling`.
**Should it exist:** yes. Specs 001 and 002–011 all write `platform.audit` and nothing reads it outside the brand. This is the reader spec 001 FR-016/FR-018 reserves for `reasoner`. Its shape (consumer per source, pure `Keep`/`Stored`, registry keyed by HMAC) is the plan's and fits the repository's service pattern.
**Read:** spec.md, plan.md, tasks.md, data-model.md, contracts/{routes,ingest,recording}.md, research.md (R8, *Found during implementation*), PERF.md. Read in full: every main source under `services/audit/src/main`, the `Routes`, `Layout`, `Manifest`, `Realm` and `Render` hunks, both manifests, CI and Justfile hunks, and the `wallet`, `players` and `responsible-gambling` main hunks. Tests read in full: `LeakSuite`, `StoredSuite`, `ReidentifySuite`, `RoutesSuite` hunk, players' `PublishersSuite` hunk. The other suites were skimmed for shape, sleeps and ports. Code opened outside the diff: `contract/.../personal/Personal.scala` (`PersonalFields.names`), `PlayerEntity` `Erased` handler and consent commands, wallet `AccountFacts`/`AccountState`, and ankka's workflow skill (timers are a cluster singleton). Not reviewed line by line: the feature bindings beyond their ignore lists, `IngestPerf`, spec prose beyond the requirements.
**Size:** 139 files, +7,617 −127 (about 5,900 Scala, 1,700 docs). That is well past where defect-finding holds up. The repository builds a spec per branch, so a split is not demanded. The neighbour changes (wallet, players, responsible-gambling mappings and their tests, ~550 lines) were separable and would have been reviewable on their own ahead of the service.
